## Opening {#opening}

I am here to help you understand where you are in your life, and to find paths toward healing and movement through life's labyrinths, through Soul Reading, Inner Knowing, and Messages from Deeper Realms.

## Who I Am {#who}

I am Zvi Ben-Ami, a spiritual practitioner and therapist working with the deeper realms of the self, reading messages of the soul through the palm.

There are moments when words and logic are not enough. Something within us calls for deeper listening, and another way of knowing.

In a session with me, the palm is an opening. Through observation and touch, a space of inner knowing unfolds, often bringing surprising and profound insights into a person's life: their path and purpose, their strengths, patterns and places where something has become blocked, and possibilities waiting to open.

From there, the process may continue through deep conversation and experiential work with conscious dreaming (Lucid Dreaming), inner imagery and the artist's path. There is no single method or fixed path; the work develops from the person and from what emerges in the process.

> It is a space of listening beyond words, allowing us to see ourselves differently and opening the possibility for a process of healing, movement, growth and fulfillment.

## What I Offer {#offer}

A personal one-to-one session that begins with a Soul Reading through the palm and opens into a deeper, experiential encounter.

Through the palm, messages and insights emerge about your path: the strengths and qualities you have brought with you into this world, your inner core and the purpose seeking expression in your life, as well as patterns, challenges and places where something may have become blocked. What emerges can sometimes be surprising in its depth and accuracy, allowing you to see yourself from a new perspective.

The palm reading is not the end of the session. It is an opening. From there, we may continue through conversation and experiential work with conscious dreaming (Lucid Dreaming), inner imagery and the Artist's Path, according to the person and what emerges in the process.

> This is not fortune-telling. The session is about what is present within you now and the possibilities that are seeking to open.

My intention is for you to leave with something of value that you can carry forward: a deeper understanding, a sense of direction, and an opening toward a process of healing, growth and fulfillment.
